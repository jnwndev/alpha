# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 20 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **P1 Alpha** (“the Bot”), operated by **jjxn2** (“we”, “us”, or “our”), processes information when you use the Bot through Discord.

## 1. Information We Process

P1 Alpha processes only information reasonably necessary to provide eBay listing monitoring.

This may include:

* Discord user IDs.
* Discord channel IDs.
* Discord server identifiers where required.
* Messages or commands sent to the Bot.
* Message content necessary to detect eBay URLs.
* eBay item IDs and listing URLs.
* Listing titles, prices, currency, availability, sale information, and end times.
* Tracking status and timestamps.
* Limited technical logs required for troubleshooting and security.

P1 Alpha does not intentionally store the complete contents of ordinary Discord conversations.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

* Register eBay listings for monitoring.
* Associate tracked listings with the requesting Discord user and channel.
* Check listings for changes.
* Send requested Discord notifications.
* Restore tracking after Bot restarts.
* Maintain security and reliability.
* Diagnose technical problems.

## 3. Message Content

P1 Alpha may access message content where necessary to detect eBay links submitted in channels where the Bot has permission to operate.

Message content is not used for advertising, profiling, or training artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information or Discord API data.

Information may be processed by third-party services necessary to operate P1 Alpha, including:

* Discord.
* eBay.
* Hosting or infrastructure providers used to operate the Bot.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Tracking information may be stored in P1 Alpha's database so monitoring can continue after Bot restarts.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, or meet legal requirements.

## 6. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their Discord account.

Requests can be sent to:

**[p1alpha.bot@gmail.com](mailto:p1alpha.bot@gmail.com)**

Please include your Discord user ID so the relevant records can be identified.

## 7. Third-Party Services

P1 Alpha depends on third-party services including Discord and eBay.

These services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

P1 Alpha is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by eBay Inc.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when P1 Alpha's functionality, legal requirements, or third-party platform requirements change.

The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
